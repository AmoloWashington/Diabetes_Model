Meal model Dalla Man et al. 2007

A 12-state nonlinear ODE model of gastric emptying, glucose absorption, hepatic production, insulin-dependent utilisation, renal excretion and β-cell secretion. It is the core of the UVA/Padova simulator, accepted by the FDA in 2008 as a substitute for pre-clinical animal trials.

β-cell mass dynamics Topp et al. 2000

A slow–fast dynamical system with a physiological stable node, a saddle threshold and a pathological attractor. It shows why the rate of insulin-resistance onset decides whether β-cell compensation succeeds.

Insulin sensitivity Bergman 1979

The minimal model of IVGTT kinetics, with nonlinear least-squares estimation of SI, SG and p2 and their precision.

Leak-free risk model

269 of the 520 records in the public symptom dataset are exact duplicates. We evaluate with grouped cross-validation, bootstrap CIs and calibration, and show how much naive evaluation overstates accuracy.
